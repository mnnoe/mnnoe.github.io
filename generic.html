Hello! I am
Micah Noe

I do my best work in the middle of change: a platform migration, a program doubling in size, an organization adjusting to new leadership. My role is to make that change land, both for the systems and for the people who rely on them. I started in retail technology at SPS Commerce, moving from customer support into quality engineering and then program management. There I identified the need for, scoped, and drove a migration that moved a major retailer’s vendor base to upgraded encryption, and led delivery of an integration connecting 30+ retailers’ suppliers to an analytics product.

Since then, much of my work has been with nonprofits and mission-driven organizations. I’ve coordinated a cohort-based Jewish bridge-building program while participation doubled, supported executives through leadership transitions, and guided a nonprofit’s move from Microsoft Copilot to Claude Enterprise, defining and testing the AI tools its staff use every day. I also founded a company focused on training and knowledge management for nonprofits, and I chair the tech committee for a Holocaust education nonprofit’s board.

Along the way I earned a data science certificate and a paralegal certificate and worked on civil rights cases. Each taught me the same lesson: systems only matter if the people on the other end can use them. Whether I’m writing Python, running a sprint, or building a program calendar, I spend much of my time translating between the people who build systems and the people who use them.

I’m fluent in Spanish and based in Eastern Time, though I am flexible to meet your time zone needs. If your organization is navigating a migration, a reorganization, or a program that has outgrown its systems, I’m glad to talk. You can reach me by email from the Contact page. I look forward to hearing from you!
